# Surface brief: portfolio home (index.html)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and freelance clients, weighted equally (see PRODUCT.md). Their job: see shipped work fast, then email or open the resume.
User pins (third redesign): "we want simple but also we want motion design to be moving on scroll triggers"; "motion graphics are moving upon scroll"; drop the 3D background. The earlier steer still stands: minimal colors.
Memorable moment: scattered blocks snap together into a solid 3x3 build as you scroll the opening; at the end, a cobalt block grows to fill the screen and becomes the contact page.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A plain page whose graphics are scrubbed to the scroll position. Simple flat blocks and lines move exactly as far as you scroll and run backwards when you scroll up; nothing plays on its own. Blocks fit the idea of building things and keep the page simple. It refuses ambient motion, 3D scenes, film props, fade-up-on-every-section entrances and cards.

OWN-WORLD: A flat off-white ground (#f5f5f3), ink (#111111), gray text (#5f5f5b), hairlines at 12% ink, and one cobalt (#2340ff) used only by the motion graphics and the final contact field. Host Grotesk is the only typeface, at 400, 500 and 600. The graphics are square blocks with a 4px radius and 2px lines, with no gradients or shadows.

STORY: Name, tagline, intro and actions, with nine scattered blocks that assemble as you scroll. Then selected work: a heading that slides across, with each screenshot unmasking and settling as it scrolls in. Then other projects, an about section with a recognition timeline that draws itself as you scroll and a stack whose words gather into line, and finally contact, when a cobalt block grows to fill the viewport.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The name is large at left, with the tagline, intro, "See the work" and the resume link under it. On the right (below the text on phones) sit nine blocks scattered at angles in ink and cobalt. Scrolling pins the opening and snaps them into a 3x3 grid.

FORM: User-pinned direction (no roll; the earlier concept seed is seed:b95bc18f37285e0b). Signature interaction: every graphic is scrubbed with GSAP ScrollTrigger. Under reduced motion every graphic shows its final assembled state, with no pin and no scrub.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
